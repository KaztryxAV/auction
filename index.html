📜 Official Auction Rules

  • 🏛️ The Grand Gallery Table: You sit with three realistic collectors: Maya (Left), Arthur (In Front), and Leo (Right)—or invite up to 3 friends!
  • ✋ Your Bidding Card: You hold your #01 Bidding Card in your right hand. Whenever you or anyone else bids, the bidder raises their card by hand!
  • 👑 10 Silly Lots & 1 Most Expensive: 10 hilarious treasures (from Screaming Rubber Chickens to Flying Slippers) magically appear. One of them is the legendary 24k Golden Plunger or Neon Crocs!
  • ⏱️ Halftime Pause (Only After Lot #5): Right after the 5th item is sold, there is a 1-Minute Selling Pause (resell items for 70%) and a 1-Minute Trading Pause.
  • 🏆 Winning & Tie-Breaker: Most items after Lot #10 wins! If tied on item count, whoever has more money left wins!

HOW IT WORKS

Rules of the Grand Gallery

✨

1. 10 Silly Lots

10 hilarious items (from Cold 3AM Pizza to Screaming Chickens) appear under the glass dome. One of them is a legendary Crown Item like the 24k Golden Plunger!

✋

2. Raise Your Card

You hold your #01 Bidding Card in your right hand. Click your card or press Space to smoothly raise your hand and place a bid.

⏱️

3. Lot #5 Halftime

Only once—right after the 5th item is sold—there is a 1-Minute Selling Pause (resell items for 70%) and a 1-Minute Trading Pause.

🏆

4. Cash Tie-Breaker

The player with the most items after Lot #10 wins! If two players finish with the same number of items, whoever has more money left wins.
