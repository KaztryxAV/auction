📜 Official Auction Rules

  • 🏛️ The Grand Gallery Table: You sit with three randomized rival collectors (Left #03, In Front #02, and Right #04) while the Grand Auctioneer strikes the Royal Hammer in the back of the hall—or invite up to 3 friends!
  • ✋ Your Bidding Card: You hold your #01 Bidding Card in your right hand. Whenever you or anyone else bids, the bidder raises their card by hand!
  • 👑 10 Silly Lots & 1 Most Expensive: 10 hilarious treasures (from Screaming Rubber Chickens to Flying Slippers) magically appear. One of them is the legendary 24k Golden Plunger or Neon Crocs!
  • ⏱️ Halftime Pause (Only After Lot #5): Right after the 5th item is sold, there is a 1-Minute Selling Pause (resell items for 70%) and a 1-Minute Trading Pause.
  • 🏆 Winning & Tie-Breaker: Most items after Lot #10 wins! If tied on item count, whoever has more money left wins!
